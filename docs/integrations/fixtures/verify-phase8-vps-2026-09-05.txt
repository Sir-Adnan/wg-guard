Defect found by the real-host gate
----------------------------------
- A peer-only `awg syncconf` cleared the owned interface private key on the pinned kernel backend,
  preventing all client handshakes. WG-Guard now snapshots the complete live `[Interface]`
  section, composes the desired peer set beneath it, applies that full configuration, and
  verifies that interface state is byte-identical afterward. Missing or malformed key-bearing
  interface state fails closed. Unit, integration, and exact real-host traffic regressions pass.
- The pinned kernel's `showconf` also synthesizes an `AdvancedSecurity` peer line even though the
  kernel setter does not store it and the pinned userspace transport rejects it. The cross-backend
  verifier removes only that known phantom line; WG-Guard continues to classify the field as
  unsupported and does not expose it.
